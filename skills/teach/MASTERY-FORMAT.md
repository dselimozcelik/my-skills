# MASTERY.md Format

`MASTERY.md` is the current evidence index. It prevents repeated beginner explanations without inflating mastery from mere exposure.

```markdown
| Concept | Level | Last verified | Sanitized evidence | Next proof required |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Spring container and beans | 1 | YYYY-MM-DD | LR-0004: traced bean selection and injection | Diagnose a missing/ambiguous bean |
```

Use levels `0` through `3` exactly as defined in `SKILL.md`. Keep concepts narrow enough that one piece of evidence supports the row. Never store proprietary code, paths, schemas, or names.
