# LEARNING-QUEUE.md Format

```markdown
# Learning Queue

| Concept | Priority | Why it may matter | Prerequisites | Revisit trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

Use `required soon` or `later` for concepts discovered during repository work but not currently blocking. Keep entries sanitized and portable. Remove or update an entry when it becomes an active learning gate; do not use the queue as proof of mastery.
